Frequently asked questions

Quick support answers

Where is the support assistant?

Open the purple chat button fixed to the bottom-right of any public page. Replies are generated locally and are not transmitted or stored.

How do I request account access?

Open the Get Account page from the main screen, review the guidance, and use the official support bot for legitimate account-access information.

What should I include in an error report?

Include the route, exact error message, browser name, approximate time, and non-sensitive steps that reproduce the issue.

Does the chatbot use an external AI API?

No. The current assistant is a local rule-based help system. It does not send your messages to an AI provider or backend.

Which information must stay private?

Never send passwords, OTPs, card PINs, CVVs, recovery codes, private keys, or complete authentication tokens.

Security reminder

CYRUS PANEL support should never ask you to publish a password, OTP, private key, card PIN, CVV, recovery code, or complete authentication secret.
