About CYRUS PANEL

CYRUS PANEL v2.0
Unified Device Management Platform

CYRUS PANEL is a full-featured IoT device management platform that connects multiple Firebase Realtime Databases into a single unified pool. Administrators can add Firebase connections, and all users get access to every device across all connected databases.
