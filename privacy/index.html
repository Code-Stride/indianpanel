Privacy

Privacy information.

Important data-handling considerations for this deployment. This is general information, not a professionally reviewed privacy policy.

Effective: August 11, 2026Version 1.0

1. Information entered

Information submitted through connection forms may be transmitted to configured third-party services. Do not submit credentials or personal information unless you are authorized and understand where the deployment processes it.

2. Browser preferences

Theme, card density, layout, and privacy-mode preferences may be stored locally. Invitation dismissal may be remembered for the current browser-tab session.

3. Third-party services

The interface may link to or communicate with platforms such as GitHub, Cloudflare, Firebase, and Telegram. Their independent terms and privacy practices apply.

4. Sensitive information

Never submit passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, card PINs, or private keys unless a legitimate authorized workflow requires it and appropriate protections exist.

5. Site-owner responsibility

The operator is responsible for an accurate jurisdiction-specific policy, lawful basis, retention rules, security controls, and contact details before production use.
